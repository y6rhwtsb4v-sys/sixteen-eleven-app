Common questions

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone? Open Settings in the app, then Sixteen Eleven Study, and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I cancel the Study subscription? On iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Google Play, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions. You keep Study until the end of the period you paid for.

My highlights and notes are gone. They live only on your device. If you kept a backup (Settings, Download a backup), use Restore from a backup. The app also keeps automatic restore points.

Reading reminders do not arrive. Turn on Remind me to read in Library, Tracker, then check that notifications are allowed for Sixteen Eleven in your phone’s settings. No reminder comes on a day you have already met your goal.

Can I listen without a connection? Yes: open Downloads in the Library to keep the narrated books on your phone.
